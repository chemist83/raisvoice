An interactive sound visualization experience

This site turns sound into a living visual simulation. Import an MP3 or WAV file to observe how audio frequency and amplitude can be represented as movement through air molecules. (New updates keep coming!)

The visualizer uses your browser's audio tools to analyze the sound locally. Imported audio files can also be reopened from My Computer on this device. You can access the audio files that you upload unless you delete browsing datas. Windows 7 Aero 版本 XP200 型号

Explore the relationship between sound, frequency, amplitude, and motion.
